0. BAZANI YARATISH VA TIZIMGA TAYYORLASH (CREATE TABLE)

Quyidagi topshiriqlarni bajarish uchun avvalo bazani va asosiy jadvallarni yaratib oling:

1. 'Department' (Bo'limlar) jadvalini yarating:

   * dept_id (INT, PRIMARY KEY)

   * dept_name (VARCHAR(50), NOT NULL)

   * location (VARCHAR(50))

2. 'Employee' (Xodimlar) jadvalini yarating:

   * emp_id (INT, PRIMARY KEY)

   * first_name (VARCHAR(50))

   * last_name (VARCHAR(50))

   * salary (DECIMAL(10,2))

   * hire_date (DATE)

   * job_title (VARCHAR(50))

   * dept_id (INT, FOREIGN KEY -> Department.dept_id)

3. 'Customer' (Mijozlar) jadvalini yarating:

   * customer_id (INT, PRIMARY KEY)

   * city (VARCHAR(50))

   * country (VARCHAR(50))

   * status (VARCHAR(20))

4. 'Orders' (Buyurtmalar) jadvalini yarating:

   * order_id (INT, PRIMARY KEY)

   * order_date (DATE)

   * total_amount (DECIMAL(10,2))

   * customer_id (INT, FOREIGN KEY -> Customer.customer_id)
