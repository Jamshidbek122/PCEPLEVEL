7. Dastur nima chiqaradi?

   try:
       print(10 / 2)
   except ZeroDivisionError:
       print("Xato")

8. Dastur nima chiqaradi?

   try:
       print(10 / 0)
   except ZeroDivisionError:
       print("Xato")
   print("Tugadi")

9. Dastur nima chiqaradi?

   try:
       x = int("abc")
   except ValueError:
       print("A")
   else:
       print("B")

       print("C")
